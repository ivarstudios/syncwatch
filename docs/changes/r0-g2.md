## 1. An urgent problem around the start of quiet hours is never reported (hunch 4, VERIFIED)

Root cause: `overnight` built the "opened and resolved during the night" list from `HistoryBetween(start, end)` with the filter `OpenedAt >= start`. A finding that opened at 21:58, became due at 22:03 (quiet, so no ping) and resolved at 23:00 was therefore in no list: not pinged, not pending (never pinged), not a blip (opened before quiet start). The same filter also lost a finding that became due during the night and resolved after quiet end but before a late summary.

What changed (`notifier.go`, `overnight`, new `announced`): candidates are now every urgent occurrence that opened or resolved between quiet start and the summary. One is listed when it became due (opened for the primary check's debounce) before quiet end, lasted at least that debounce, and was never announced. "Announced" is read from the finding's record: pinged at or before the occurrence resolved (a re-open that carried `PingedAt` over is the same, already announced incident, which `TestFlapKeepsSinglePing` guards). The old `pendIDs` exclusion is subsumed by this rule. Still-open findings that became due during the night are in `due` as before and are pinged by the summary.

User-visible: the overnight summary lists every urgent finding that was due at some moment during quiet hours and was never pinged — still open (with the mention) or resolved (in "Opened and resolved during the night", with its times). Test: `TestOvernightListsDueBeforeQuietResolvedDuring`; the late-summary case is in `TestLateOvernightLabelsDaytimeFindings` (item 7).

README: "Notifications" → Quiet hours bullet (what the 07:00 summary contains).

## 2. A reminder time inside the evening part of quiet hours never fires (hunch 5, VERIFIED)

Root cause: `reminder` computes `at` as today's reminder time and sends in `[at, at+12h]` on non-quiet ticks. With `22:00-07:00` and `reminder_time: 23:00` every tick until midnight is quiet, and from 07:00 on `at` (today 23:00) is in the future, so the reminder is never sent. `Validate` accepted the setting.

What changed (`config.go`, the `reminder_time` hunk in `Validate`): when quiet hours are on (parseable and not equal ends), a reminder time inside the window is rejected if it could never be sent: the window ends on the next day (`t >= end`, the evening part of a window crossing midnight) or more than 12 hours after it (the notifier's reminder window). Error text: `notifications.reminder_time must be outside quiet_hours: a reminder at 23:00 would never be sent with quiet hours 22:00-07:00`. It applies to YAML load, CLI import, the web settings save and the web import, since all go through `Validate`.

DECISION: only reminder times that can never be sent are rejected. A time inside the window that the notifier does send at quiet end (the morning part, e.g. `06:00`, or a daytime window such as `09:00-17:00` with `12:00`) stays accepted, because `Validate` also runs at startup (`config.NewHolder`) and rejecting it would stop an existing install from booting, for a setting that works. The 12-hour part covers windows longer than 12 hours (e.g. `20:00-14:00` with `01:00`).

User-visible: saving or importing such a configuration fails with the error above. An install that already has one (the reminder never fired) will fail to start until it is changed; the error says why. Test: `TestReminderInsideQuietHoursRejected` (in `notifier_test.go`, since the 12 h window is the notifier's).

README: configuration example (`reminder_time` comment), "Notifications" → Daily reminder bullet.

## 3. Ignored or snoozed findings appear in the overnight "opened and resolved" list (hunch 7)

Root cause: the blip loop had no snooze filter (`dueRecords` has one).

What changed (`notifier.go`, `overnight`): an occurrence is skipped when the finding is ignored or snoozed at the time of the summary or at the time it resolved. A snooze that expired before the summary and was pruned (`PruneSnoozes` runs every 6 h) can no longer be seen; such a blip is still listed.

The second half of hunch 7 (the blip debounce is the primary check's, the ping debounce the shortest urgent check's) is left as is: a history row carries only the primary check, and for the single-check H/S findings that are urgent the two are the same.

User-visible: ignored and snoozed findings no longer appear in the overnight summary. Test: `TestOvernightSkipsIgnoredAndSnoozed`.

README: "Notifications" → "Snoozed … and ignored findings never ping" bullet (add: and are not listed in the overnight summary).

## 4. Daytime flapping pings the role every cycle; resolution has no hysteresis (hunch 8)

Root cause: `resolved` sent the "resolved" message at the next non-quiet tick after the engine resolved the record, so the engine's flap protection (carrying `PingedAt` over when the old record is pinged and not resolved-notified) only covered a re-open before that tick. 6 min down / 4 min up for 2 h gave 12 pings with the mention and 12 resolved messages.

What changed (`notifier.go`, new `resolveSettle`, `settled`; `resolved` and `overnight`): a resolved finding is reported (as a "Resolved" message, or in the summary's "Resolved during the night") only once it has stayed resolved for `min(its debounce, 5 min)`; `debounce` is the same shortest-urgent-check rule as for pings. Until then it stays pending, so a re-open inside that window carries `PingedAt` over and continues the same incident: no new ping, no resolved message. The same flap for 2 h now gives one ping and nothing else; "resolved" goes out 5 minutes after the link stays up; a later outage is a new problem with a new ping.

Coordination with fixer c: the change only filters what `PendingResolved()` returns by `ResolvedAt` and marks only the settled ones with `MarkResolvedNotified`; it does not depend on how the resolved mark is stored. If fixer c's change keeps `ResolvedAt` on pending records and the carry-over of `PingedAt` for a re-open before the mark, nothing here needs to change.

User-visible: "Resolved" messages arrive up to 5 minutes (or the check's debounce, if shorter) after the problem clears. Tests: `TestResolveHysteresisDuringFlapping`; `TestDebounceBatchResolve` updated.

README: "Notifications" → Resolved bullet (delay and flapping), the urgent ping bullet (a problem that comes back within that window is the same incident).

## 5. The weekly report at startup says all servers are down, and is caught up immediately (hunch 9)

Root cause: a missed slot is caught up at the first tick, before the collectors have reached the servers; `HealthLine` counted every status other than `up`, including `unknown` (not contacted yet), as down.

What changed (`notifier.go`): the notifier records its first tick (`started`); `reminder` and `weekly` return without setting their marker while `checked` is false: some enabled server (`cfg.Servers` minus `Disabled`) is missing from the snapshot or still `unknown`, and less than `startupGrace` (5 min) has passed since the first tick. A server's status leaves `unknown` only after a completed full check (collector.go:173-178) or after `down_after_failures` failures, so "first result" means a real answer. `HealthLine` now has three groups: `3 servers OK`, or `1/3 servers OK (SOL down; SIRIUS not yet checked)`; `unknown` is never called down. `cert-changed` and the manager's "no API key" states still count as down, as before.

User-visible: after a start inside a missed weekly or reminder window the message waits for the first results (at most 5 minutes). The health line (weekly report, dashboard overview, `/api/v1/status` `health`) says "not yet checked" for servers that haven't answered. The overnight summary is not gated: it lists findings that were already open for their debounce. Test: `TestScheduledMessagesWaitForFirstResults`.

README: "Notifications" → Weekly report bullet (health line format), "API" paragraph (health line), possibly "Start and restart" notes.

## 6. The same problem is announced twice within an hour each morning (hunch 15)

Root cause: the reminder listed every open urgent finding pinged before the reminder time, so the 07:00 summary's pings were repeated at 08:00, and a 07:59 ping at 08:00.

What changed (`notifier.go`, `reminder`): findings pinged later than one hour before the scheduled reminder time are skipped (`PingedAt > at − 1h`); this includes being listed as "still open" in the overnight summary, which marks them pinged. The hour is measured from the scheduled time, not the tick, so the 30 s tick jitter doesn't decide whether a 07:00 summary and an 08:00 reminder overlap. Exactly one hour before is not skipped (`TestQuietHoursAndReminder` pins that boundary with minute-exact ticks).

User-visible: with the defaults the 08:00 reminder no longer repeats the 07:00 summary or a ping from the last hour; findings announced earlier are still reminded. Test: `TestReminderSkipsRecentlyAnnounced`.

README: "Notifications" → Daily reminder bullet.

## 7. The overnight summary can go out late and include daytime findings as "new" (hunch 14)

Root cause: `overnight` is retried for up to 12 h after quiet end (channel failing, back-off) and then used `dueRecords(now)`, which includes findings that became due after quiet end.

What changed (`notifier.go`, `overnight`, `OvernightMessage`): due findings are split by the time they became due (`OpenedAt + debounce`): before quiet end → "New and still open", at or after → a new section "New since quiet hours ended (n)", appended as the fourth section so existing section positions don't move; the Discord payload drops empty sections, so the usual summary looks as before. The mention and the colour consider both groups; both groups are marked pinged. The daytime-quiet-window case from hunch 14 is unchanged (the summary is sent whenever a quiet window ends).

User-visible: a summary sent late labels what became due after quiet hours ended. Test: `TestLateOvernightLabelsDaytimeFindings` (also covers a finding due during the night and resolved before the late summary, from item 1).

README: "Notifications" → Quiet hours bullet.

## 8. HealthLine plural/singular

Root cause: fixed-format strings (`%d servers OK`, `%d PCs`, `%d errors, %d warnings, %d suggestions`).

What changed (`notifier.go`, new `plural`): `1 server OK`, `1 PC`, `1 error`, `1 warning`, `1 suggestion`; the ratio form keeps `0/1 servers OK`. Test: `TestHealthLineWording`; `TestWeeklyReport` updated.

User-visible: weekly report summary, dashboard overview, `/api/v1/status` `health`.
